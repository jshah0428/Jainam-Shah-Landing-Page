Building at the intersection of logic and creativity.

Fourth Year Student @ New Jersey Institute of Technology

Hello! I’m Jainam Shah, a fourth-year Computer Science student at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, studying at the Albert Dorman Honors College with minors in Mathematics and Business.

My journey into tech began during Code Week with a Minecraft-inspired Scratch project—an experience that ignited a lasting passion for software engineering. Since then, I’ve grown increasingly interested in machine learning, low-latency systems, cloud computing, and other cutting-edge technologies.

What excites me most is the intersection of logic and creativity that programming offers. I’m always looking for new challenges and opportunities to grow as a developer. Outside of the digital world, I enjoy biking, working out, and traveling. During the summer, I often play pickup basketball at local parks.

I’m a strong believer in life-long learning and continuous personal development, and I enjoy connecting with others who share that mindset. Feel free to reach out through my socials above - I’d love to chat!
